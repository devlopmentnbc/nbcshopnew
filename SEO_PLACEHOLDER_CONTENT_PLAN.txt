B4. "$" prices on listing pages
    (sheet: "Listing cards show $ while the cart shows LKR")
    Cause: this is the site's country-based currency feature, not
    placeholder text. Visitors from outside Sri Lanka (detected by IP)
    get USD prices everywhere - listings, cart and checkout use the same
    session currency. The audit crawler ran from outside Sri Lanka, so it
    saw "$". For a Sri Lankan visitor all prices are LKR.
    OPTIONS (your decision - see questions below):
      a) Keep as is (overseas customers see USD), but always show LKR to
         search engine crawlers (Googlebot etc.), so Google indexes LKR
         prices. (recommended)
      b) Show LKR to everyone and remove the USD feature.
      c) Leave unchanged.
